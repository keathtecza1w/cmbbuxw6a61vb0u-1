<p><p>Metal bellows seals are specialized sealing devices used in various industries to prevent leakage of fluids or gases. The market is divided into two main types: stationary and rotary seals. Stationary seals are designed for applications where the components remain fixed, providing a reliable seal against pressure and environmental conditions. In contrast, rotary seals are used in dynamic applications where one component rotates, offering flexibility and resistance to wear while maintaining a robust sealing performance in diverse operating conditions.</p></p>
